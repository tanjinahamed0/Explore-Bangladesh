## Limitations

- “Exploring Srimangal by Bicycle” uses a verified tea-garden scene because no reliable Commons photograph of cycling specifically in Srimangal was available.
- “Island Life” on Saint Martin’s uses a verified coastal boat scene rather than a village lane because no reliable village photograph was available.
- “Kochikhali” uses a verified Sundarbans mangrove sunset because no reliable still photograph of Kochikhali itself was available.
- “Hiron Point” uses a general mangrove scene for the attraction card; the destination hero uses the specifically named Hiron Point photograph.
- “Musapur Closer” uses a verified photograph of the Musapur Closer river area in Companiganj; the closure structure itself is not in the frame.
- Some generic Bangladesh landscapes are chosen for section imagery; destination and attraction photos are matched to their named places whenever a verified image was available.
